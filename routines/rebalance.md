# rebalance

## When
After every log, "what is left?", "what should I eat for lunch?", "plan today".

## Read
Today's Day, Goals, Pantry.

## Steps
1. Remaining = target minus running totals: kcal, protein, fat, carbs. Say it after every log; a macro over its max: say so, suggest a lighter next slot.
2. Open slots: no entry yet, in order breakfast, lunch, snack, dinner, minus slots removed in chat ("no snack today" writes nothing).
3. Suggest only on request. "Plan today" before the first log: every open slot in full. After a log: the next slot in full, one rough line for the rest.
4. Pantry only: protein first, then grain, vegetable, fruit, fat. `until` today or tomorrow first, with a note. Servings when the Food has one, else grams rounded to 10 g.
5. Protein split across slots is your judgment unless the user says. Pantry short of protein: say so, one or two non-pantry options.
6. Evening, lunch still open: ask "did you skip lunch?". `~` on the totals of an estimated Day. Nothing carries over.

## Write
Nothing. Plans live in the chat.

## Reply
After a log: one line, what is left. On request: the suggestion, amounts and macros per item, one total per slot.
